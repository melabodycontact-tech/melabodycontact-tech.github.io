about mela

A woman-owned family business in New York, making body care by hand in small batches.

mela makes three products: a body balm, a face oil and a hair oil. Each one is made from organic oils, butters and botanicals, mixed by hand in New York and poured into amber glass.

where it started

It started with the body balm. The recipe has been in our family for generations, and it was the first thing we wanted to share. The face oil and the hair oil came after. We made them the same way, with a short list of organic ingredients, each one there for a reason.

how we make it

We keep batches small so that what you receive is fresh. The base oils are cold-pressed, and nothing we make contains synthetic fragrance. Amber glass helps protect the oils from light.

You can also find us on Etsy, where we sell as melabotanical.
