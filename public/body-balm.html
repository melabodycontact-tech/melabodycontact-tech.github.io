Body Balm

Luscious melt-to-the-touch balm for neck to toe softening and moisture protection.

Our signature balm, made from a recipe that has been in our family for generations. Jojoba and sunflower seed oils soften the skin, while cocoa butter and beeswax help hold moisture in. It warms and melts the moment it touches skin.

There is no added fragrance, just the faint scent of cocoa and beeswax, which makes it a good fit for sensitive skin.

how to use

Apply after a shower as an all-over moisturizer. Use it daily on hands and lips, or anywhere that feels dry.

ingredients

100% organic: Jojoba oil, Beeswax, Cocoa butter, Sunflower seed oil, Rosemary extract.

care
