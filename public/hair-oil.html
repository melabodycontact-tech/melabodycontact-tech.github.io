Ritual Hair Oil

Lightweight oil for smoothing strands and caring for the scalp.

A blend of jojoba, camellia, argan and amla berry oils with rosemary, lavender and peppermint essential oils. Use a few drops on the ends for shine, or work a full dropper into the scalp as a weekly treatment before you wash.

how to use
daily

Apply a few drops to the ends for moisture and shine.

weekly

Massage a full dropper into the scalp as a nourishing pre-wash treatment. Leave it on for an hour or overnight, then shampoo as usual.
