questions

shipping

Orders ship from New York to addresses in the US. Shipping is $5, and free on orders over $50.

returns and exchanges

We accept returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery. The buyer pays return shipping, and items need to come back in their original condition. Send us a note above to start a return.

payment

Checkout runs through Stripe and accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored on our site.

storage

Keep your balm and oils somewhere cool and out of direct sun. The amber glass helps, but heat and light can still change natural oils over time.

sensitive skin

Each product page lists every ingredient. The Body Balm has no added fragrance and contains beeswax. The face and hair oils contain essential oils. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, try a small amount on your inner arm first.
